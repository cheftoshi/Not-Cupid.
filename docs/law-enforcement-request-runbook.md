# Law-enforcement request handling

Operator-approved September 27, 2026. Public protocol: /law-enforcement.
Owner: the NotCupid operator, through match@notcupid.com. This is a manual
process, not continuous monitoring, an automated disclosure service, or a
guaranteed response-time commitment.

1. Record receipt, case reference, asserted deadline, request type and sender
   in a restricted case record. Never put requests, user exports, or identifying
   case material in Git, public logs, or analytics.
2. Verify agency and officer through an independently obtained official
   contact channel. Do not rely solely on email domain, attachments, links,
   or a phone number supplied in the request.
3. Identify the accounts precisely. Review jurisdiction, legal authority,
   requested record categories, dates, service requirements, and notice
   restrictions. Consult qualified counsel for uncertain process, content
   disclosure, cross-border demands, or emergency exceptions. Do not treat a
   preservation request as authority to disclose.
4. For valid preservation obligations, promptly arrange a narrowly scoped,
   access-restricted preservation of existing records with counsel/technical
   assistance. Record the applicable deadline and review/extension date.
   There is no automatic legal-hold feature: verify preservation actually
   occurred and account for deletion jobs before confirming it. Do not
   promise recovery of unavailable records or suspend unrelated deletion.
5. Assess an emergency's stated threat, urgency, requested information and
   legal basis. Our inbox is not a substitute for emergency services.
6. Record the notification assessment, any legal prohibition or safety reason
   for delay, and any appropriate review date. Do not send a user notice or
   release records without explicit operator approval of that action.
7. Review a proposed export for scope, account identity and unrelated data.
   Use an appropriately secured delivery channel to the verified recipient;
   never grant database access or send secrets. Record approval, what was
   disclosed, recipient, legal basis, time and delivery method.
8. Review retention when the matter or preservation period ends, following
   legal obligations and the privacy policy. Document the decision.

Reference for preservation and required disclosure:
https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=(title:18%20section:2703%20edition:prelim)
This checklist does not replace case-specific legal advice. No email or user
data disclosure is authorized by publishing the protocol.
